Task 4

Feature cards, twice over

The same six cards laid out with Flexbox and then with CSS Grid. No media queries are used for the card wrapping — flex-wrap and auto-fit handle it.

1 — Flexbox

display: flex; flex-wrap: wrap; with flex: 1 1 260px on each card. Cards grow to fill the row, so a lone card on the last row stretches full width.

2 — CSS Grid

grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(250px, 1fr)). Columns are equal by definition and rows stay aligned, so a lone card on the last row keeps its column width instead of stretching.

Named areas let one card span two columns and two rows at a wide viewport, and collapse back to a single stack on mobile — laid out in two dimensions at once.
