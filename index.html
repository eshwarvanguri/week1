About me

Turning ideas into clean, usable interfaces

I'm a developer who enjoys the part of the job where a rough sketch turns into something people can actually click. My focus is the frontend: semantic markup, a layout that holds up on every screen size, and JavaScript that stays readable six months later.

Week one of the Traidix Tech program covered the fundamentals end to end — the request/response cycle, semantic HTML5, Flexbox and Grid, responsive design, ES6+ JavaScript, DOM events, and Git. Everything on this site was built while working through those topics.

Outside of coursework I'm usually reading up on accessibility or rebuilding an interface I liked to see how it was put together.
